# InstaStation: Instant Parcel Tracking Dashboard
📦 "For every student waiting in line, InstaStation offers a direct path from delivery to doorstep."

**InstaStation** is a university-based parcel tracking and management system, developed as a final year project at **Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah (UMPSA)**. It provides a centralized platform where students and staff can manage parcel deliveries with ease and efficiency.

Most students receive frequent deliveries during their studies, yet traditional collection systems are often slow and disorganized. InstaStation solves this problem by offering real-time tracking, instant notifications, and scheduled pickup options — all through a user-friendly dashboard.

### 🎯 Project Goals
- Reduce delays and confusion in parcel collection
- Minimize lost packages
- Enhance the overall student experience

### 🔑 Key Features
- 📍 **Live parcel tracking**  
- 🔔 **Instant notifications on arrival**  
- 📅 **Pickup scheduling system**  
- 👨‍💼 **Staff dashboard for logging and updating parcel status**  
- ✅ **Centralized records and smooth communication**

By streamlining parcel intake and retrieval, InstaStation supports both students and university staff in managing high volumes of parcels with minimal effort.

> "From doorstep to desk, without the mess — InstaStation delivers clarity."
